# Guided curriculum

The guided path goes from God’s Plan of Salvation through Know the Mass and Sacraments into Living the Faith. The Body of Christ lesson bridges into Preparing for Mass, and Dismissal leads to What Is a Sacrament, and Matrimony leads into New Life in Christ. The former Church collection URL redirects to God’s Plan of Salvation.

A future Catechism section will explore the apostles and their successors, the four marks, and the Church’s mission and worship in more depth.
